How to Download

  1. Copy the video link from YouTube, Facebook, TikTok or Instagram.
  2. Tap Paste (or paste it in the box).
  3. Tap the big Download button.
  4. Pick a quality from the list (HD, 720p, etc.).
  5. The video downloads to your device.
